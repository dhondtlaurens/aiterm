# Workflow-agnostic Operator workers

## Goal

A signal can run an ordinary prompt or an installed workflow. A worker that needs an answer ends its turn with a final `Question:` paragraph that the Operator can read and answer. Headless helpers may run in the worker's shell or in a keyed background tab. Their output belongs to the caller. Their hooks, status lines and exit cannot change the worker's identity, finish its turn or wake the Operator.

Claude, Codex and Antigravity are the three signal workers. PI stays basic-tier and participates in the helper tests. Workflow choices belong in the signal's existing free-text prompt. The separate issue with background shells in sessions adopted after a daemon restart is outside this change.
